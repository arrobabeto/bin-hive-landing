## Who is responsible for your data

Arrobabeto Media, the project of Alberto (@arrobabeto) in Mexico, is responsible for the personal data you share on the Bin Hive waitlist.

## What data we ask for

Only what we need to let you know about the launch: your name, your email address, how you would use Bin Hive (creator or agency) and the language of the page where you signed up.

## What we use it for

- Confirming your spot on the waitlist.
- Letting you know when we open spots and sharing Bin Hive news.
- Understanding which kinds of profiles are interested, to prioritize the product.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

## Where it is stored

We use MailerLite as our email provider to manage the list. MailerLite processes the data on our behalf under its own security measures.

## Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to its use and withdraw your consent at any time:

- Use the unsubscribe link included in every email.
- Or reply to any email we send you stating which right you want to exercise.

We will respond within 20 business days, in line with Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties.

## Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will publish the new version on this same page with its update date.
